Shipping Policy

Fast, reliable delivery to your doorstep

Free Shipping

We offer free shipping on all orders over $50. Orders below this threshold incur a standard shipping fee of $5.00 within major cities and $10.00 for remote areas.

Shipping Options

  • Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days
  • Express Shipping: 2-3 business days (additional $15.00)
  • Same-Day Delivery: Available in select cities for orders placed before 12 PM

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking information once your order has been shipped.

International Shipping

We currently ship to select international destinations. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. Please contact us for more details.
